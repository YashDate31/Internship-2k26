Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about shopping, shipping, and returns

How long does delivery take?
Standard delivery takes 1-3 business days across major cities in India. Express same-day delivery is available for eligible pin codes selected at checkout.
What is your 30-day return policy?
If you are not satisfied with your purchase, you can initiate a return request from your Profile page within 30 days of delivery. Free doorstep pickup is provided with full refunds processed within 48 hours.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major UPI applications (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit & Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay), Net Banking, and Cash on Delivery (COD).
How can I track my live order?
Once your order ships, a SMS and email containing a tracking link will be dispatched. You can also view real-time tracking from your ShopKing Profile page under "My Orders".
