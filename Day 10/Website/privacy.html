At a Glance: Your Data Rights

ShopKing respects your privacy. We store only essential account details required to process your orders and guarantee lightning-fast shipping. We never sell your personal contact information or payment details to third parties.

1. Information We Collect

To provide seamless shopping experiences, we collect information in the following categories:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, and delivery addresses provided during registration or checkout.
  • Transaction Details: Order history, billing totals, and payment status receipts (handled via PCI-DSS compliant gateways).
  • Technical & Analytics: IP address, device type, browser model, and anonymized page analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

We strictly use your collected information for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Fulfilling orders, processing payments, and arranging courier deliveries.
  • Sending critical order notifications (SMS/Email shipping updates).
  • Preventing fraudulent transactions and ensuring account authentication security.
  • Providing 24×7 customer care support and handling return requests.

3. Data Encryption & Security

We deploy bank-grade 256-bit SSL encryption across all website communications. Payment processing is conducted through accredited third-party payment gateways. We never store raw credit card numbers or UPI PINs on our servers.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

ShopKing uses browser local storage and cookies to remember items added to your Shopping Cart, maintain session login state, and preserve user preferences across browsing sessions.

5. Your Rights & Choices

You have full control over your data. You may inspect, update, or delete your account information at any time via your Profile page. To request full deletion of your historical data, contact privacy@shopking.com.

6. Refund Policy & Terms of Service

Orders placed on ShopKing are covered under our 30-Day Money Back Guarantee. Returned items must be in original condition. Refunds are credited back to the original payment source within 48 hours of return inspection.
